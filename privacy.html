EMS Narrator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026
Short version: there is no EMS Narrator account, server, analytics, or tracking. The developer never receives anything you enter. The one time data leaves your device is when you tap Generate Narrative with your own Anthropic API key, and then it goes straight from your device to Anthropic.

What the app is

EMS Narrator helps EMS clinicians turn tapped call events into a draft narrative. It is a documentation aid. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, or replace clinical judgment or agency policy. You are responsible for reviewing and editing every narrative before use.

What is stored on your device

  • Your customized protocol items and medic unit names.
  • Your free-narrative counter and subscription status.
  • Your Anthropic API key, if you add one. In the iOS app it is stored in the iOS Keychain.

The event log you build during a call is held in the app's memory only. It is not written to device storage by the app.

What is sent off your device

Generate Narrative with an API key. The app sends the list of events you tapped (item names, timestamps, and any successful/unsuccessful outcome you marked) and the selected unit name to Anthropic's API, using your own key. This request goes directly from your device to Anthropic; it does not pass through any server operated by the developer. Anthropic's terms and privacy policy govern how they handle it (anthropic.com/privacy). Usage is billed to your Anthropic account.

Without an API key, or offline. The app builds a basic narrative locally. Nothing leaves your device.

ChatGPT option. The app copies a prompt to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it into another service yourself, and that service's policies then apply.

Do not enter patient identifiers. The app is built around tapping standard items and does not ask for names, dates of birth, or addresses. Keep it that way when you add custom items or unit names.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription status are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Compliance

This policy describes how the app behaves. It is not a statement that the app meets HIPAA or any agency requirement for your use. Follow your agency's policies on where patient information may be entered and sent.

Children

EMS Narrator is intended for EMS professionals and is not directed to children.
